## Evidence behind the CV

My CV explains the experience. This GitHub keeps the proof behind it: real production cases, executable technical work, troubleshooting evidence, customer communication, QA, integrations, incident handling and reusable documentation.

**Fastest review:** open the [Evidence Registry](https://zpthanos.github.io/zpthanos/) and search a role, technology, symptom or customer situation. Try `API`, `checkout`, `incident`, `customer success`, `technical account manager`, `unhappy customer`, `QA`, `security`, `onboarding` or `NATO`.

### Customer-facing scope

| Area | Evidence from my work |
|---|---|
| **Customer Support** | End-to-end issue ownership from intake to reproduction, investigation, escalation and verified closure across customer-facing production systems. |
| **Customer Success** | Onboarding, practical training, post-launch support, adoption, handover, relationship recovery and turning recurring friction into better guidance. |
| **Technical Account Ownership** | Primary point-of-contact responsibilities across ~30 client accounts: requirements clarification, expectation setting, incident communication, coordination with developers/QA/specialists and business-continuity awareness. |

> **How I work a problem:** Customer report → clarify → reproduce → investigate → resolve or escalate → verify → communicate → document.

The goal is not to know every answer immediately. It is to reduce uncertainty quickly, collect useful evidence, keep the customer informed and leave a better path behind.
